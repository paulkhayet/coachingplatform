Your questions,
answered.

Get to know the platform we're building.

Can I use schedulefa.st today?

schedulefa.st is in development. Join the early-access list for product updates and opportunities to try it as it takes shape. There is no payment or software access at signup.

Is it for solo coaches or teams?

Both. A solo coach handles the business and coaching in one place. Teams can split responsibilities between an owner, administrators, and coaches, with owner-defined permissions and shared business settings.

Can I keep my own packages and policies?

That's central to the plan. Configure program length, frequency, session formats, payment arrangements, cancellation rules, expiration, and makeups around your business.

Will clients need an account just to inquire?

The planned experience lets families complete intake, initial-call booking, commercial steps, and checkout without a portal account. Client account setup follows a successful coaching payment.

How will shared coach pools work?

A business can choose to enable a pool of eligible coaches. A client's purchased allowance can be used across that pool, while staff access to private records remains governed by permissions. Pricing access is reserved for owners and administrators.

What about my calendar and video calls?

Connected calendars, meeting links, and payment services are part of the planned workflow. Specific providers and integration details are still being defined; we'll share those as development progresses.
